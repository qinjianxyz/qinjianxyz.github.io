Pulse Macro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Pulse Macro (“Pulse”, “the App”) is an iOS application that displays public market and macro data (charts, named math indexes, curves, and related tools). This policy describes what information the App handles.

Summary

  • Pulse is built to show public data and transparent formulas. It does not provide financial advice.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data the App processes on device

Depending on features you use, the App may store locally on your device (including App Group storage shared with the Pulse widget):

  • App preferences (for example display options, alert and morning-brief settings)
  • Watchlist symbols you choose
  • Cached public market series and widget snapshots for offline / faster display
  • Habit / consecutive-open counters used only for free-tier tool limits

This information stays on your device unless you back up the device with Apple (iCloud Backup / computer backups) under Apple’s terms.

Purchases and Apple services

If you buy Pro or redeem an offer code, billing and account identity for that purchase are handled by Apple through App Store / StoreKit. Pulse does not receive your full payment card details. See Apple’s privacy policy for App Store transactions.

Network requests

To show public series, the App may request data from public sources such as FRED, U.S. Treasury, CoinGecko, and similar documented endpoints (see in-app source footers). Those requests typically include standard technical information (IP address, device/user-agent) received by those providers under their own policies. Pulse does not attach a Pulse account profile to those requests.

Notifications

If you enable optional local notifications (for example sparse alerts or a morning numbers brief), scheduling happens on device. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support page at https://qinjianxyz.github.io/pulse/support.html or email the publisher listed on the App Store product page.
