Pulse Macro — Support

Public-data macro dashboard for iOS

Pulse Macro shows public rates, commodities, debt metrics, and transparent math indexes (formula + inputs + as-of). It does not provide buy, sell, or hold advice.

Help

  • In the app: Settings shows version stamp, data-status meanings, and Free vs Pro tools.
  • Pull to refresh on Home to reload public series.
  • TestFlight builds: install/update from Apple’s TestFlight app.
