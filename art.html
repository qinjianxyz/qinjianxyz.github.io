JIAN QIN / POETRY & VISUAL ART

Poems,
seen anew.

A bow in the snow. A light sheltered in a sleeve.
Dawn over the sea.
Some poems stay with me as images.

I’m exploring Chinese poetry through images. Four of these works draw on classical poems; the fifth accompanies my own poem, A Star Within the Sleeve.

Literature first. Art second. Engagement third.
