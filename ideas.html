PROJECT IDEAS / 项目灵感

从你的兴趣出发。Start with what interests you.

有人想做软件，有人想让小车动起来。孩子自己的点子，也值得认真做。An app, a moving robot, or an idea your child can’t stop talking about. There is room to make it their own.

AI 与软件AI & software

做一个你自己也会用的产品。Build a product you would use yourself.

01

邮件与日程助手Inbox & planning assistant

整理待办、准备回复、提醒重要的事。Sort tasks, prepare replies, and surface what matters.

02

社团与跑团平台Club & running community

发布活动、报名、管理成员与照片。Events, sign-ups, members, and shared photos.

03

小店接单系统A shop ordering system

从菜单、下单到订单进度。Menus, orders, and status tracking.

04

自己的知识助手Your own knowledge assistant

从自己的资料中找到答案，并给出出处。Find answers in your documents, with sources.

传感器、自动化与机器人Sensors, automation & robotics

先让它读懂环境，再让它行动。First sense the world. Then respond.

01

桌面环境站Desk environment station

温湿度、实时面板、断线提醒。Temperature, humidity, a dashboard, and stale-data alerts.

02

会转向的小装置A responsive mechanism

传感器控制舵机，加入角度限制与停止按钮。A sensor-controlled servo with angle limits and a stop control.

03

避障小车Obstacle-avoiding rover

测距离、控制转向，记录成功和失败的路线。Measure distance, steer, and evaluate successful and failed runs.

04

植物照料助手Plant care assistant

观察土壤变化，先提醒，再探索低压自动控制。Monitor soil conditions, send a reminder, then explore low-voltage control.

区块链与去中心化应用Blockchain & decentralized apps

用链上的记录，做出实际的功能。Turn onchain records into useful features.

01

NFT 会员授权NFT membership & authorization

钱包签名、验证持有、开放会员内容；转让后重新检查权限。Sign in, verify ownership, and unlock member content; re-check access after a transfer.

02

测试网收款台Testnet checkout

创建订单、核对付款、生成收据。Create orders, verify payments, and issue receipts.

03

比特币交易追踪Bitcoin transaction tracker

看懂输入、找零、手续费与确认状态。Inspect inputs, change, fees, and confirmations.

04

数字参与凭证Digital participation credentials

设计发行、验证与撤销规则，讨论什么应该放在链上。Design issuance, verification, and revocation, and decide what belongs onchain.
