提示 / Hints

需要时再展开 / Open if stuck
  1. 没有勾选的动作应失败。默认拒绝，而不是默认允许。 Unchecked actions should fail. Deny by default, do not allow by default.
  2. 公开职位和申请材料不是同一类数据。 Public job listings are not the same as applications.
  3. 雇主只能改自己的职位；其他雇主、候选人、访客都不行。 Only the owning employer updates that job.

任务 / Task

给模型温室的低压风扇写控制：热了要开；用回差减少抖动；传感器过期、故障和急停在本演示中安全关断。 Control a low-voltage greenhouse fan: turn on when hot; use hysteresis to reduce chatter; stale readings, faults, and emergency stop fail safe to off in this demo.

没有连接真实硬件。这里的“故障则关”只是本练习的选择，不是通用工业安全设计。 No real hardware is connected. Safe-off on fault is a choice for this exercise, not a universal industrial safety design.

    提示 / Hints

    需要时再展开 / Open if stuck
    1. 先让 32 ℃ 能开风扇，18 ℃ 能关掉。 Turn on at 32 °C and off at 18 °C first.
    2. 27 ℃ 附近的小波动不是过热；开启值应更高，并留出回差。 Noise around 27 °C is not heat; leave a gap between on and off.
    3. 过期、故障和急停不要当成普通温度。急停解除后从“关”重新判断。 Stale, fault, and stop are not ordinary temperatures. After stop, re-evaluate from off.

    提示 / Hints

    需要时再展开 / Open if stuck
    1. 连上钱包只说明你能使用这个模拟地址，不说明你现在持有通行证。 Connecting a wallet is authentication, not entitlement.
    2. 转让后要查当前账本。打开缓存开关，是为了复现旧持有人被误放行。 Recheck the ledger after transfer. The cache toggle exists to reproduce stale-owner access.
    3. 未连接、没有 Token、旧持有人，应分别被拒绝。 Deny unconnected wallets, missing tokens, and former holders as different cases.
