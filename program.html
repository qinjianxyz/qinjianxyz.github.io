AI 招聘平台AI-powered recruiting platform

查看路线 / View project

以招聘平台为起点，完成职位发布、搜索、申请和 AI 信息整理。也可以根据兴趣改成商家接单或社团平台。Develop a recruiting platform with job posting, search, applications, and AI-assisted summaries. Adapt the project into an ordering system or club platform if that fits your interests.

  1. 定下第一个版本Define the first version

    选一个用户、一件要完成的事，画出核心页面。Choose one user and one task. Sketch the essential screens.

  2. 让内容保存下来Save the important things

    建立账号和数据，让刷新页面不会丢失内容。Add accounts and storage so work survives a refresh.

  3. 打通核心流程Connect the main journey

    浏览、搜索、申请或下单，每一步都有结果。Make browsing, searching, and applying or ordering work from end to end.

  4. 加一个有用的 AI 功能Add one useful AI feature

    选择摘要、搜索助手或内容整理，用例子检查是否真的有帮助。Choose summaries, assisted search, or content organization. Test whether it helps.

  5. 让别人来试Let someone else try it

    检查手机显示、权限、空输入和网络失败，部署预览。Check mobile layout, permissions, empty inputs, and failures. Deploy a preview.

  6. 交付你的作品Present your product

    用新账号完成核心流程，解释设计，并独立修改一个功能。Complete the journey with a fresh account, explain your choices, and change one feature yourself.

完成时 / Your finished work

一个可访问的产品原型、源代码、演示和启动说明。A working product prototype, source code, a demo, and setup instructions.

AI 邮件与待办助手AI inbox & task assistant

查看路线 / View project

把邮件整理成待办，找到需要回复的内容，生成由你确认的回复草稿。Turn messages into a useful task list, find what needs a reply, and prepare drafts for you to review.

  1. 先选一个最烦的环节Choose the part you want to fix

    从示例邮件开始，写清楚哪些要回复、哪些只要留档。Start with sample messages. Decide which need a reply and which can be filed.

  2. 做出收件箱与待办视图Build an inbox and a task view

    展示发件人、摘要、截止日期和原始邮件，能找到每条待办的出处。Show the sender, summary, due date, and original message. Keep every task traceable.

  3. 加上 AI 分类和草稿Add AI sorting and drafts

    保留原文，标出不确定的判断，草稿由你修改确认。Keep the originals, flag uncertainty, and let the user edit every draft.

  4. 用不那么整齐的邮件测试Try the awkward cases

    测试重复邮件、没有日期的请求、广告和邮件里夹带的指令。Test duplicates, missing dates, ads, and instructions embedded in message text.

  5. 接入自己的工作习惯Fit it into your routine

    先用导入方式；需要连接邮箱时，再单独配置账户授权和权限。Begin with imports. If you want a live mailbox connection, configure account permissions as a separate step.

  6. 用一组邮件完整演示Walk through a complete batch

    展示整理、待办、草稿审核的全过程，能撤回错误操作并说明数据去向。Demonstrate sorting, tasks, and draft review. Show how to undo a mistake and explain where data goes.

完成时 / Your finished work

一个可运行的助手、示例邮件、测试记录和自己的使用说明。A working assistant, sample messages, test notes, and a guide for your own workflow.

环境监测与自动控制Environmental monitoring & control

查看路线 / View project

测量温度、湿度或土壤变化，在网页里看到读数，再让灯光或小舵机作出反应。Measure temperature, humidity, or soil changes, view the readings on a dashboard, and make an LED or small servo respond.

  1. 选择一个要测量的变化Choose something to measure

    先在模拟器里试，再按确认的清单准备开发板、传感器和 USB 线。Start in a simulator, then use an agreed parts list for the board, sensor, and USB cable.

  2. 读到第一个数值Read the first value

    对照接线图接上低压传感器，把读数显示出来。Follow a wiring diagram for a low-voltage sensor and display its first reading.

  3. 让读数值得相信Check whether readings make sense

    用已知条件对照，记录噪声、误差和断开传感器的情况。Compare known conditions and record noise, error, and a disconnected sensor.

  4. 做一个能看懂的面板Build a useful dashboard

    显示数值、单位、时间和最近的变化，数据过期时要看得出来。Show units, timestamps, and recent changes. Make stale data visible.

  5. 加一个小动作Add a small response

    让 LED 或小舵机按阈值响应，加入手动停止及传感器失效时的安全状态。Trigger an LED or small servo at a threshold, with a manual stop and a safe state when sensing fails.

  6. 演示一次完整实验Demonstrate the experiment

    改变环境，观察读数和动作，说明接线、阈值与误差。Change a condition, observe the reading and response, and explain the wiring, threshold, and measurement limits.

完成时 / Your finished work

一个低压传感器装置、可视化面板、接线图、实验记录与代码。A low-voltage sensing device, dashboard, wiring diagram, experiment notes, and code.

NFT 会员与访问授权NFT membership & access control

查看路线 / View project

用钱包证明身份，用链上的 NFT 决定访问权限。亲手做出会员页面，测试转让、到期和撤销后的变化。Use wallet signatures to prove account control and an onchain NFT to determine access. Build a members-only page and test transfers, expiry, and revocation.

  1. 先写会员规则Define the membership rules

    明确谁发行、谁能持有、能否转让，以及过期或撤销时怎么办。Decide who issues and holds passes, whether transfers are allowed, and how expiry or revocation works.

  2. 发行练习通行证Issue practice passes

    在本地或测试网络创建 Token，给练习账号发放。Create tokens on a local or test network and issue them to practice accounts.

  3. 证明账户由你控制Prove control of the account

    通过有时效的签名挑战验证账户，而不只是填写一个地址。Use an expiring signature challenge instead of trusting a typed wallet address.

  4. 接上会员页面Connect the members’ page

    服务端检查规则后开放内容，拒绝未授权访问。Enforce the rules on the server before returning members-only content.

  5. 检查权益变化Test changes in access

    用两个账号检查转让、到期或撤销后的访问结果。Use two accounts to test access after transfer, expiry, or revocation.

  6. 解释你的会员系统Explain your membership system

    演示发行和验证，并说明 Token 本身不自动创造法律上的所有权。Demonstrate issuance and access checks, and explain why a token alone does not establish legal ownership.

完成时 / Your finished work

一个会员验证应用、练习 Token、权限测试和明确的会员规则。A membership app, practice tokens, access tests, and explicit membership rules.

  4. 把数据做成页面Build the tracker view

  5. 处理等待和错误Handle waiting and errors

    测试无效编号、未确认交易、找零和服务暂时不可用。Test invalid IDs, unconfirmed transactions, change, and service outages.

  6. 解释一笔完整交易Explain the whole transaction

    从发送到确认演示一次，说清楚钱包、交易和区块各做什么。Demonstrate a payment through confirmation and explain wallets, transactions, and blocks.

适合谁 / Starting point

能完成签名登录和服务端授权，理解合约状态。Can implement signed sign-in and server authorization and explain contract state.

项目步骤 / Project milestones
  1. 选定规则：会员治理、凭证或测试收款Choose rules for membership governance, credentials, or test payments
  2. 设计合约与链下数据的边界Design the boundary between contracts and offchain data
  3. 实现事件读取与状态同步Read events and synchronize state
  4. 处理重复、等待、撤销和服务中断Handle duplicates, pending states, revocation, and outages
  5. 测试权限攻击与故障恢复Test permission attacks and recovery
  6. 整理威胁模型、实验和使用说明Document the threat model, experiments, and operation
成果检查 / What you’ll demonstrate
  • 重启或重复事件不造成重复发放或重复记账Restarts and repeated events do not duplicate issuance or accounting
  • 能说明中心化依赖、隐私取舍和未解决风险Explain centralized dependencies, privacy tradeoffs, and remaining risks

测试网收款、可撤销凭证、会员投票；题目与范围单独确认。Testnet checkout, revocable credentials, or member voting; scope is agreed individually.

开始之前，你可能想问。A few questions before you begin.

没有编程基础，能参加吗？Can I start without coding experience?

可以。先聊聊你的基础和兴趣，选择适合的起步项目。有经验的学员可以直接进阶，不必从头重复。Yes. We choose a starting project based on your experience and interests. Experienced students can begin further along.

用 AI 写代码，怎样知道孩子真的学会了？How do you check learning when students use AI?

不只看程序能不能运行。评审时，学员要讲清关键设计、自己修改一个功能，并检查正常与出错时的表现。我会根据这些实际表现给反馈。A running program is only part of the review. Students explain key decisions, change a feature, and test normal and failure cases. My feedback is based on what they can demonstrate.

孩子有自己的想法，可以做吗？Can my child bring an original idea?

欢迎。我们一起把想法拆成能完成的第一版，再确定需要学什么。也有准备好的项目可选，不需要先想好题目。Absolutely. We work out a first version and the skills it needs. Prepared projects are also available; students do not need to invent a course topic.

家长怎样参与？How can parents take part?

青少年由家长报名并签字。家长欢迎一起看教材、参加答疑和作品评审。我们用具体作品和进度交流，不只说“学得不错”。Parents enroll and provide consent for teens. You are welcome to use the materials, join office hours, and attend reviews. We discuss actual work and progress.

8 周之后呢？What happens after the mentoring period?

教材和自己的作品继续保留。如果还想一起做项目，可以按月续共享指导；需要单独深入解决问题，也可以预约一对一。Keep the materials and your work. You can continue shared mentoring by the month, or book an individual session for more focused help.

机器人要买什么？区块链要用真钱吗？What equipment or funds do these projects need?

Blockchain projects use a local chain or testnet; buying cryptocurrency is not required. Equipment, subscriptions, API usage, and hosting costs are discussed first.
