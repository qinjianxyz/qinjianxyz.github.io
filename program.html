AI · 全栈软件AI · Full-stack software

LEVEL 1 / 初学

做出第一个应用Your first app

一个能添加、搜索和保存内容的小应用。A small app that adds, searches, and saves information.

适合谁 / Starting point

不要求编程基础；会使用电脑和文件夹即可。No coding experience required; comfortable using a computer and folders.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 选一个用途：社团活动、阅读清单或职位看板Choose a purpose: club events, reading lists, or a job board
  2. 借助 AI 做页面，理解输入与输出Use AI to build a page and trace inputs and outputs
  3. 把页面和服务连接起来Connect the page to a server
  4. 保存数据，检查刷新和重启Save data and check reloads and restarts
  5. 找到并修复一个错误Find and fix a bug
  6. 请别人试用，修改并演示Ask someone to try it, improve it, and demonstrate
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 刷新后数据仍在，搜索真的会筛选Data survives a reload and search actually filters
  • 解释一次请求经过哪里，并独立修改一个功能Trace a request and independently change a feature

在自己的兴趣题材上换内容、改规则。Change the content and rules to match your interests.

LEVEL 2 / 进阶

做一个别人能用的产品A product others can use

一个有账号、权限、完整操作流程和 AI 功能的网站。A website with accounts, permissions, a complete user workflow, and an AI feature.

适合谁 / Starting point

能运行和修改小应用，理解页面、服务和数据的关系。Can run and modify a small app and explain page, server, and data flow.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 画出两类用户和一次完整操作Define two user roles and a complete workflow
  2. 实现登录、数据结构和权限Implement sign-in, data structures, and permissions
  3. 完成发布与申请，或下单与处理Complete posting and applying, or ordering and fulfilment
  4. 接入 AI 摘要或分类，保留人工确认Add AI summaries or classification with human review
  5. 测试越权、重复提交和 AI 错误Test unauthorized actions, duplicate submissions, and AI mistakes
  6. 部署试用，记录反馈并修订Deploy for testing, collect feedback, and revise
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 另一个人独立完成一次业务流程Another person completes the workflow without help
  • 用户不能改别人的数据；失败有提示且能恢复Users cannot alter others’ data; failures are visible and recoverable

招聘网站、社团平台、小店接单或知识助手。Job boards, club platforms, shop orders, or knowledge assistants.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through

  3. 管理文件、上下文和任务状态Manage files, context, and task state
  4. 加入确认、预算和取消机制Add approval, budget, and cancellation controls
  5. 测试错误输入、工具失败和恶意指令Test bad input, tool failures, and hostile instructions
  6. 比较版本表现，记录成本并交付Compare versions, record costs, and deliver
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 在未见过的任务上评估成功率和成本Evaluate success rate and cost on unseen tasks
  • 未经确认不发送邮件或改重要文件；失败可追踪No email sends or important file changes without approval; failures are traceable

邮件处理、资料整理、社团运营；AI Agent 是实现这些功能的工具。Inbox work, document organization, or club operations, using AI agents where useful.

传感器 · 自动化 · 机器人Sensors · Automation · Robotics

LEVEL 1 / 初学

读懂环境，让灯响应Sense the room and control a light

一个温湿度面板和按规则亮灭的指示灯。A temperature and humidity dashboard with a rule-controlled light.

适合谁 / Starting point

不要求硬件经验；可以先在模拟环境开始。No hardware experience required; begin in simulation.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 认清传感器、控制板和输出设备Identify the sensor, controller, and output
  2. 读取并理解温湿度数值Read and interpret temperature and humidity
  3. 连接网页，记录带时间的读数Connect a dashboard and timestamp readings
  4. 实现阈值与停止命令Implement thresholds and a stop command
  5. 检查失效、断线和旧数据Check sensor failure, disconnection, and stale data
  6. 导出实验记录并解释结果Export experiment records and explain results
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 正常、停止、失效三种情况都有记录Record normal, stopped, and failed states
  • 分清模拟读数与实物读数，解释两种阈值Distinguish simulated and physical readings and explain two thresholds

桌面环境站、植物状态提醒、房间温度记录。Desk monitors, plant status reminders, or room temperature logs.

LEVEL 2 / 进阶

让装置按规则行动Build a responsive mechanism

适合谁 / Starting point

能读取传感器、使用面板并处理停止与失效。Can read sensors, use a dashboard, and handle stop and failure states.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 选定用途，画结构和接线Choose a use and draw the structure and wiring
  2. 确认供电、负载和动作范围Check power, load, and motion range
  3. 校准传感器与舵机位置Calibrate sensor readings and servo positions
  4. 加入手动与自动模式Add manual and automatic modes
  5. 测试断线、抖动与停止后的行为Test disconnects, jitter, and behavior after stopping
  6. 重复实验，比较稳定性Repeat experiments and compare reliability
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 动作不超过约定范围，模式切换可解释Motion stays within agreed limits and mode changes are explainable
  • 在三种不同输入条件下重复成功Repeat successful behavior under three input conditions

转向底座、互动装置、模型温室；先确认具体设备。A rotating platform, interactive mechanism, or model greenhouse; agree on hardware first.

LEVEL 3 / 深入

做一台会调整动作的小机器人Build a robot that adjusts its actions

一台能感知距离、调整路线并记录表现的小车。A rover that senses distance, adjusts its route, and records performance.

适合谁 / Starting point

能独立完成传感器与执行器项目，具备基本调试经验。Can complete a sensor-actuator project and troubleshoot it.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 定义场地、任务和通过标准Define the course, task, and success criteria
  2. 组装底盘并测试电机与停止方式Assemble the chassis and test motors and stopping
  3. 测量距离，校准速度与转向Measure distance and calibrate speed and steering
  4. 实现感知与动作反馈Implement sensing and action feedback
  5. 比较不同路线和干扰条件Compare routes and disturbance conditions
  6. 用实验记录改进设计Improve the design using experiment records
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 多次完成约定路线，报告失败次数和原因Complete repeated runs and report failures and causes
  • 能根据新障碍修改策略；明确感知与机械限制Adapt to a new obstacle and explain sensing and mechanical limits

避障、循线或遥控协作；视觉识别作为后续扩展。Obstacle avoidance, line following, or remote cooperation; vision is a later extension.

LEVEL 1 / 初学

看懂一笔链上交易Understand an onchain transaction

一个能显示地址、余额、交易和确认状态的查询工具。A viewer for addresses, balances, transactions, and confirmation status.

适合谁 / Starting point

不要求投资或钱包经验；只使用练习网络。No investing or wallet experience required; use practice networks only.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 区分钱包、账户、网络和资产Distinguish wallets, accounts, networks, and assets
  2. 使用练习账户发起一笔交易Send a transaction from a practice account
  3. 读取交易与确认状态Read transactions and confirmation status
  4. 用整数单位计算金额与费用Calculate amounts and fees in integer units
  5. 比较错误地址、失败与等待状态Compare wrong addresses, failures, and pending states
  6. 做成可查询的页面并解释证据Build a query page and explain the evidence
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 能解释网络、金额、手续费和确认的区别Explain network, amount, fee, and confirmation
  • 不会把等待或失败的交易当作成功Do not treat pending or failed transactions as successful

以太坊测试网查询；比特币输入、找零和手续费可单独深入。Ethereum testnet queries; Bitcoin inputs, change, and fees are an optional deeper route.

LEVEL 2 / 进阶

用 NFT 管理会员权限Build NFT membership access

一个用钱包签名登录、按 NFT 持有情况开放内容的网站。A site with wallet sign-in and content access based on NFT ownership.

适合谁 / Starting point

能做基础网页，理解账户和交易；可先补软件基础。Can build a basic page and explain accounts and transactions; bridge software gaps first.

  1. 定义会员能访问什么Define what membership unlocks
  2. 部署练习 NFT 并发给测试账户Deploy a practice NFT and issue it to test accounts
  3. 用一次性消息验证钱包签名Verify wallet signatures with one-use messages
  4. 服务端读取持有情况并保护内容Check ownership server-side and protect content
  5. 测试转让、撤销、过期与重放Test transfer, revocation, expiry, and replay
  6. 解释链上与网站各自负责什么Explain the responsibilities of chain and website
怎样算做会了 / How we check
  • 仅改浏览器状态不能解锁内容Changing browser state cannot unlock content

社团会员、活动通行证或创作者内容；不涉及投资承诺。Club membership, event passes, or creator content, without investment promises.

LEVEL 3 / 深入

设计完整的链上应用Design a complete onchain application

一个有完整状态流程、异常处理和审计记录的去中心化应用。A decentralized app with complete state transitions, failure handling, and an audit trail.

适合谁 / Starting point

能完成签名登录和服务端授权，理解合约状态。Can implement signed sign-in and server authorization and explain contract state.

你会经历 / What you’ll work through
  1. 选定规则：会员治理、凭证或测试收款Choose rules for membership governance, credentials, or test payments
  2. 设计合约与链下数据的边界Design the boundary between contracts and offchain data
  3. 实现事件读取与状态同步Read events and synchronize state
  4. 处理重复、等待、撤销和服务中断Handle duplicates, pending states, revocation, and outages
  5. 测试权限攻击与故障恢复Test permission attacks and recovery
  6. 整理威胁模型、实验和使用说明Document the threat model, experiments, and operation

  • 重启或重复事件不造成重复发放或重复记账Restarts and repeated events do not duplicate issuance or accounting
  • 能说明中心化依赖、隐私取舍和未解决风险Explain centralized dependencies, privacy tradeoffs, and remaining risks

测试网收款、可撤销凭证、会员投票；题目与范围单独确认。Testnet checkout, revocable credentials, or member voting; scope is agreed individually.

可以从合适的级别直接开始，也可以跨方向补基础。每次先确定一个项目的成果与指导范围，不要求一次学完所有级别。Enter at the right level and bridge skills across fields when needed. We agree on one project and its mentoring scope at a time; you do not need to take every level.

小型机器人 / 传感器SMALL ROBOTICS / SENSORS

做一个会感知、会提醒的小装置。Build a small device that senses and responds.

测量温度、湿度或土壤变化，在网页里看到读数，再让灯光或小舵机作出反应。Measure temperature, humidity, or soil changes, view the readings on a dashboard, and make an LED or small servo respond.

  1. 01选择一个要测量的变化Choose something to measure

    先在模拟器里试，再按确认的清单准备开发板、传感器和 USB 线。Start in a simulator, then use an agreed parts list for the board, sensor, and USB cable.

  2. 02读到第一个数值Read the first value

    对照接线图接上低压传感器，把读数显示出来。Follow a wiring diagram for a low-voltage sensor and display its first reading.

  3. 03让读数值得相信Check whether readings make sense

    用已知条件对照，记录噪声、误差和断开传感器的情况。Compare known conditions and record noise, error, and a disconnected sensor.

  4. 04做一个能看懂的面板Build a useful dashboard

    显示数值、单位、时间和最近的变化，数据过期时要看得出来。Show units, timestamps, and recent changes. Make stale data visible.

  5. 05加一个小动作Add a small response

    让 LED 或小舵机按阈值响应，加入手动停止及传感器失效时的安全状态。Trigger an LED or small servo at a threshold, with a manual stop and a safe state when sensing fails.

  6. 06演示一次完整实验Demonstrate the experiment

    改变环境，观察读数和动作，说明接线、阈值与误差。Change a condition, observe the reading and response, and explain the wiring, threshold, and measurement limits.

完成时，你手里有什么What you will have at the finish

一个低压传感器装置、可视化面板、接线图、实验记录与代码。A low-voltage sensing device, dashboard, wiring diagram, experiment notes, and code.

  1. 01先写会员规则Define the membership rules
