我的经历，以及现在想做的事。My background, and what I’m doing with it.

我在北京长大，在旧金山读高中，后来在 UC San Diego 完成机械工程和数学–计算机双本科学位。在中美两种环境里生活，让我习惯从不同的角度看同一件事，也更愿意先了解一个人的处境和想法。Beijing is where I grew up; San Francisco is where I went to high school. At UC San Diego, I earned bachelor’s degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Mathematics–Computer Science. Living in both China and the US has shaped how I see things and how I work with people whose experiences differ from mine.

毕业后，我在科技公司做全栈和 AI 项目，后来成为 ZipRecruiter 的 Senior Software Engineer。我做过面向大量用户的产品，也做过自己的小型自动驾驶车。从机械结构到软件，从一个想法到真正能用，中间的工作我很熟悉。After university, I worked on full-stack and AI projects, becoming a Senior Software Engineer at ZipRecruiter. My experience includes products used at scale and personal builds such as a small autonomous vehicle. I’ve spent a lot of time on the work between an idea and something that actually runs.

25 岁时，我靠自己的积累实现了财务独立。之后，我有了更多空间去选择自己想做的事：教育、可再生能源、工业自动化，尤其是怎样改善食物生产。我想继续在中美两地实践和交流，也想亲手探索可再生家庭农场。I became financially independent at 25 through my own efforts. That gave me more freedom to choose where to spend my time. Teaching, renewable energy, industrial automation, and especially food production are things I want to pursue, through practical work in China and the US—including exploring a renewable homestead.

我愿意做教育，因为很多人在开始时缺的，只是有人帮他把问题讲明白、陪他跨过第一道坎。我可以和孩子一起做第一个网站，也可以帮成年人把工作里反复遇到的麻烦做成工具。你不用先懂一堆术语，带着问题和想法来就好。I enjoy teaching because getting started often comes down to having someone explain a problem clearly and help you past the first obstacle. That might mean a child’s first website, or a tool that takes a repetitive task out of an adult’s working day. You can start with a question or an idea; you don’t need the vocabulary first.
