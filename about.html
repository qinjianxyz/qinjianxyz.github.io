关于我，多说一点A LITTLE ABOUT ME

工程师，教育者，
农民。Engineer. Educator.
Farmer.

我是秦剑，Ray。在北京长大，高中赴美留学。UC San Diego 双学位、Cum Laude 荣誉毕业，曾任 ZipRecruiter Senior Software Engineer。现在，我开设全栈软件、机器人和区块链课程，亲自带学员做项目。I’m Ray Qin. Raised in Beijing, I moved to the US for high school, earned dual degrees at UC San Diego with Cum Laude honors, and became a Senior Software Engineer at ZipRecruiter. I now run a project-based learning program, personally mentoring students in full-stack development, robotics, and blockchain.

我的经历，现在的选择。My experience. My work today.

从北京到美国求学Education across China and the US

高中就读于旧金山湾区的 San Domenico School，获 Veritas Award——学校每届授予一名毕业生的最高荣誉，表彰学业、友谊与对社区的贡献。后来在 UC San Diego 完成机械工程与数学–计算机科学双学位，以 Cum Laude 荣誉毕业。At San Domenico School in the San Francisco Bay Area, I received the Veritas Award, the school’s highest graduating senior honor, awarded to one student per class for contributions through study, friendship, and service. I went on to earn dual B.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Mathematics–Computer Science at UC San Diego, graduating Cum Laude.

在工作中把项目做好Engineering in practice

在 ZipRecruiter 担任 Senior Software Engineer，负责全栈开发与 AI 项目。工作之外，我也做自己的项目，包括小型自动驾驶车。写软件、调硬件、排查问题，这些都是我亲手做过的事，也是我指导学员的基础。As a Senior Software Engineer at ZipRecruiter, I worked on full-stack and AI projects. My independent work includes a small autonomous vehicle. I teach from that experience: developing software, integrating hardware, debugging problems, and testing whether a system actually works.

现在，把时间花在在意的事上What I choose to work on

25 岁时，我靠自己的积累实现了财务独立，也有了更多时间做自己真正喜欢的事。教育是其中之一。我也关心可再生能源、工业自动化和食物生产，想在中美两地继续实践。跑山、练力量、研究农场，都是我生活的一部分。Becoming financially independent at 25 gave me more freedom to choose my work. Education is one of those choices, alongside renewable energy, industrial automation, and food production. I want to keep pursuing these interests in China and the US. Trail running, strength training, and farming belong in that life, too.

我亲自带你做项目Work directly with me

课程由我设计，也由我亲自指导。全栈软件、机器人、区块链都有明确的项目路线，我们会按学员的基础和兴趣调整难度。一起讨论设计、解决问题、检查成果。家长也欢迎参与，看看孩子做成了什么、下一步想挑战什么。I design the courses and mentor students myself. Each field—full-stack development, robotics, and blockchain—has a project pathway that we adapt to the student’s experience and interests. We discuss design decisions, solve problems, and review the finished work. Parents are welcome to take part and see what their child is learning.
