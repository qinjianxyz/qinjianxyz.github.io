参与性质Participation

Ray Qin 提供独立、自愿参与的项目式学习。我们未持有教育执照或认证，不授予学位或学分，不承诺就业、升学、认证、收入或投资结果。这一声明本身不构成监管豁免。Ray Qin offers independent, voluntary project-based learning. We do not hold an educational license or accreditation, and do not award degrees or academic credit. No employment, admission, certification, income, or investment outcomes are promised. This statement does not establish a regulatory exemption.

报名与排课Enrollment and scheduling

课程面向由家长报名并签字的13–17岁青少年和18岁以上成人。采用随时加入的项目学习，无需等待固定班期。首次项目包含可下载保留的教材、56天共享指导及两次作品反馈。指导从激活或付款确认时开始；到期仍可查看教材。续指导为一个自然月，手动购买，不自动扣款。首期同时接受指导的总人数拟限12位，家长可共同参与每一场答疑。Programs serve ages 13–17 through signed parent enrollment, and adults 18+. Learning is project-based with rolling entry. An initial project includes downloadable materials, 56 days of shared guidance, and two project reviews. Guidance begins on activation or confirmed payment; materials remain available afterward. Renewals are one calendar month, purchased manually with no automatic charge. The proposed initial limit is 12 people receiving guidance across programs. Parents may join every office hour.

拟定退款与改期政策Proposed refund and rescheduling policy

拟定退款方案：首次项目激活后7天内可申请全额退款；之后按剩余指导天数占56天的比例退还项目费用。月度续指导开始前可全退，开始后按剩余服务天数比例退还。已交付的一对一时间不退，提前24小时可改期。Ray取消未交付服务时退还对应费用。联系邮箱申请，由人工确认退款及访问调整；本页为经营者待确认稿，适用的法定权利不受影响。Proposed policy: request a full refund within seven days of initial project activation; afterward, refund the project fee in proportion to the unused portion of the 56-day guidance period. A monthly renewal is fully refundable before it starts, then prorated by unused service days. Delivered private sessions are not refundable; reschedule with 24 hours’ notice. Services cancelled by Ray are refunded for the undelivered portion. Email to request manual review and access adjustment. This is an owner-review draft and does not limit applicable statutory rights.
