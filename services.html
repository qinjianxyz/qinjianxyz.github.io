Fit

A good project looks like this.

Good fit

There is a real workflow or product, someone owns the decision, I can inspect representative examples, and the technical result matters now.

Not a fit

The project has no specific user or workflow, no decision owner, or no clear legal and compliance owner where one is required.

Control

The client controls repositories, cloud accounts, wallets, keys, production approvals, and regulated relationships.

Boundaries

Light Bulb Technology L.L.C. provides software engineering and technical consulting, not legal, tax, investment advisory, brokerage, custody, money transmission, or independent security audit services.
