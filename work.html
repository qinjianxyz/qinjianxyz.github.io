01 / Mechanical engineering × software

Research prototype

Anvil Sim

A simulation workbench for early design decisions.

A Mac app that brings geometry, assumptions, simulation results, and review materials together to help compare early design options.

The work

I started Anvil to build native software for mechanical engineering problems. My work includes Swift and Metal interfaces, Rust simulation workflows, and tracing results back to their inputs.

Design decision

Show the setup and limitations alongside the results so they can be reviewed together. AI can help with preparation and review; engineering judgment remains with the person using it.

Status & scopeSome simulation workflows have numerical checks within a limited scope; others are still exploratory or replay-based. They have not been validated against physical experiments or certified for engineering use.

  • Swift
  • Metal
  • Rust
  • Simulation workflows

02 / Perception × control × hardware

Past engineering project

Autonomous Vehicle

Connecting computer vision to vehicle control.

A small autonomous-vehicle project combining visual perception, embedded computing, lane following, and steering control.

The work

I worked on lane-following software running on a Jetson Nano and an object-detection pipeline using ROS2, YOLOv5, and OpenCV, integrating computer vision with the vehicle’s hardware.

Design decision

Develop perception and control on the same vehicle, where the camera, software, and actuators have to work together.

Status & scopeThis was a small experimental vehicle, not a demonstration of public-road autonomy.

  • ROS2
  • Jetson Nano
  • YOLOv5
  • OpenCV

03 / Journaling × product design

Development prototype

Life Garden

A growing garden for your everyday life.

An experimental iPhone app that connects journaling, habits, a calendar, and focus sessions to a garden that grows over time.

The work

I’m exploring a way to record everyday life that lets you revisit individual days and see a longer record take shape.

Design decision

Keep the daily details in a timeline and reflect their accumulation in the garden, giving people a reason to return to their records.

Status & scopeA prototype in development, not yet publicly released. The preview uses fictional sample entries.

  • iPhone product design
  • Journaling
  • Interaction design

04 / AI development × project continuity

Internal engineering tool

Enterprise OS

Picking up where an AI work session left off.

A lightweight tool for recording project state, preserving changes with Git, and keeping verification results, so AI-assisted work is easier to resume, review, and roll back.

The work

I use it to explore longer-running work with AI, keeping project state connected to the corresponding code changes and verification results.

Design decision

Simplify task orchestration around three needs: resuming work, preserving changes, and running the relevant checks.

Status & scopeAn internal tool. It has not been shown to improve model accuracy or to suit a wide range of teams.

  • AI-assisted development
  • Git workflows
  • Verification
  • Developer tooling

05 / Global trade × how things are made

iOS internal testing

Pulse

Exploring materials and manufacturing through trade.

Explore trade between countries, then look at the minerals, materials, processes, and equipment behind everyday goods.

The work

I’m building a native iOS app that brings together public data and manufacturing knowledge, linking trade maps to individual goods and how they are made.

Design decision

Show the source and date of the data, and connect trade relationships on the map to the relevant materials and processes.

Status & scopeCoverage is still limited. The map shows trade relationships, not shipping routes, and the data is not real-time.

  • SwiftUI
  • Data visualization
  • Public-data provenance
  • Manufacturing systems
