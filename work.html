Pulse

Finger on the Pulse of the Global Economy

把脉世界经济。看懂世界贸易格局，理解物品如何被生产。Understand global trade and how things are made.

从一个国家，到它交易的商品；从一件物品，到背后的材料、工序、设备、能源与劳动。Pulse 正在把这些关系连接成可探索的学习工具。From a country to the goods it trades. From an object to its materials, processes, equipment, energy, and labor. Pulse is being built to connect these relationships in an explorable learning tool.

01 / 世界 · World

谁向谁买卖什么？规模与依赖怎样变化？Who trades what with whom? How do scale and dependence change?

02 / 制造 · Making

原材料怎样变成产品？哪些工序和能力不可或缺？How do raw materials become products? Which processes and capabilities matter?

03 / 理解 · Understanding

把贸易、生产与宏观变化联系起来，检查解释背后的证据。Connect trade, production, and economic change. Examine the evidence behind an explanation.

我们的目标不是收集最多的数据，而是找到真正能说明现象的数据，用清晰的可视化帮助理解世界。The goal is not to collect the most data. It is to find evidence that explains what is happening—and make it understandable through visualization.

正在迭代的产品，尚未公开上架。公开数据注明来源、时期与覆盖范围；缺失保持可见。An evolving product, not yet publicly released. Public data carries sources, periods, and coverage; gaps remain visible.
