ONE QUESTION, TWO SCALES / 一个问题，两种尺度

铁矿石离开港口之后呢？What happens beyond the port?

先看贸易，再理解炼钢。两种证据相连，但不能把贸易线当作某一座工厂的供应链。Start with trade. Then understand steelmaking. These views connect ideas; they do not trace a shipment to a particular factory.

COUNTRIES → GOODS → PARTNERS

谁在向谁交易？Who trades with whom?

选择铁矿石，查看主要贸易关系、金额与来源。地图呈现的是年度贸易额，不是航线或实时货运。Select iron ore to inspect leading relationships, values and their source. The map shows annual trade value, not shipping routes or live cargo.

读图提示：截图仅画出 1,075 对关系中的前 15 对。未显示不等于没有贸易。Reading the screen: 15 of 1,075 relationships are drawn. An omitted line does not mean no trade.

MATERIAL → PROCESS → PRODUCT

矿石与废钢，为什么走不同的路？Why do ore and scrap take different paths?

矿石需要去除氧；废钢已经是金属，需要重新熔化。比较两条路线，再进入各自的工序。Ore needs oxygen removed; scrap is already metal and is remelted. Compare the two routes, then explore their steps.

工艺参考：DOE 2015。一般生产示例，不代表地图上某一笔贸易的实际去向。Process reference: DOE 2015. General production examples, not the destination of a mapped shipment.

A DIAGRAM YOU CAN QUESTION

不只记住名称，更要看懂变化。Understand the change, not just its name.

用示意图理解材料如何变化，再查看解释和来源。图形帮助理解机制，不把简化画面当成设备图纸。Use a schematic to understand a material change, then examine the explanation and source. A learning diagram explains a mechanism; it is not an equipment blueprint.

THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE / 设计原则

让证据帮助理解。Evidence that helps you understand.

来源与时间 / Source & time

贸易额、产量和工艺参考属于不同的证据。来源、年份与单位必须跟着结论。Trade values, production quantities and process references are different kinds of evidence. Sources, dates and units belong with the interpretation.

看得见的边界 / Visible limits

没有数据，就保留空缺。伙伴份额不直接等于依赖程度，示例工艺也不是供应链追踪。Keep missing data visible. A partner share alone does not establish dependence; a process example is not supply-chain traceability.
