A Star Within the Sleeve

A traveler shields a small light from the snow. Beyond the mountains, countless homes have lights of their own.

ORIGINAL POEM

A Star Within the Sleeve

Jian Qin · Contemporary

万壑沉沉夜未央，

行人袖里护微芒。

星列长空如刻尺，

手中罗盘辨八荒。

风雪弥天犹可度，

人持寸火越寒冈。

千峰万户灯如豆，

忽见人间万点光。

English translation

Translated with AI assistance. Read alongside the original Chinese poem.

Ten thousand valleys lie in darkness; night is not yet over.

A traveler shelters a faint light within his sleeve.

Stars line the vast sky like markings on a ruler;

a compass in his hand finds bearings in the boundless world.

Though wind and snow fill the heavens, the way can still be crossed.

With a tiny flame, he crosses the frozen ridges.

Across a thousand peaks, the lamps of countless homes are small as beans—

and suddenly he sees a world of lights.

READING THE POEM

Context & imagery

In Jian Qin’s original poem and oil painting, a traveler protects a small light, takes his bearings from the stars and a compass, and crosses the frozen mountains. What begins as a solitary journey leads to the discovery of countless homes, each with a light of its own.

FROM POEM TO IMAGE

The poem in the image

The light might stand for knowledge, conviction, dignity, love, or a freedom someone refuses to surrender. The traveler does not light the lamps for everyone else; he discovers that they already have lights of their own. Independence and connection coexist in this scene.
