你感兴趣的事，都可能成为一个项目。There is more than one kind of thing to build.

01 / AI + SOFTWARE

把每天的麻烦，做成好用的工具。Build tools for the things you do every day.

邮件太多、社团报名难管理、小店想接单、跑团想有自己的网站。AI 可以帮你写代码、整理信息，也可以成为产品的一部分。An inbox assistant, a club sign-up system, a shop ordering site, or a home for your running group. Use AI to build the software and give it useful abilities.

邮件助手 · 招聘网站 · 接单系统 · 个人知识库Inbox assistant · Job board · Ordering system · Personal knowledge library

02 / SENSORS + ROBOTICS

写下的代码，也可以让东西动起来。Give your code something to sense and control.

房间有多热，植物缺不缺水，小车能不能自己避开障碍？接上传感器，读到真实的数据，再试着让灯、舵机或小车作出反应。Measure your room, monitor a plant, or help a small rover avoid obstacles. Connect sensors to code, then control a light, a servo, or a moving robot.

环境监测 · 植物提醒器 · 桌面机械装置 · 避障小车Environment monitor · Plant monitor · Desktop mechanisms · Obstacle-avoiding rover

03 / BLOCKCHAIN + WEB3

不只看懂区块链，自己做一个应用。Build something that uses a blockchain.

做一个能核对付款的收款台，为社团做会员通行证，或者追踪一笔比特币交易。把钱包、交易、Token 这些概念放进自己做的项目里理解。Verify a payment, build a membership pass for a club, or trace a Bitcoin transaction. Learn wallets, transactions, and tokens by using them in your own application.

测试网收款 · 会员通行证 · 交易追踪 · 数字凭证Testnet checkout · Membership passes · Transaction tracking · Digital credentials

这里既有旗舰课程，也有可以继续探索的题目。先选一条准备好的路线，再把它做成自己的样子；拓展项目会先确认难度、设备和指导范围。These are flagship courses and ideas to explore beyond them. Begin with a prepared route, then make it your own. We agree on scope, equipment, and support before taking on an extension.
