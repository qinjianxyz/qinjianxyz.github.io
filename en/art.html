JIAN QIN / POETRY & VISUAL ART

Poems,
seen anew.

A bow in the snow. A light inside a sleeve.
Dawn across the sea.
Some poems stay with you as images.

I use contemporary visual art to find my way back into Chinese literature. These five works include interpretations of four classical poems and a poem and painting of my own.

Literature first. Art second. Engagement third.

Snow Upon the Bow and Blade

When the World Turns White

This ongoing project includes AI-assisted visual creation. Each work distinguishes the historical poet, any original contemporary writing, and the visual interpretation. English verse renderings are reading translations prepared for this site; the Chinese texts remain available throughout.
