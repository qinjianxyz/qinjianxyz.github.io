01 / Mechanical engineering × software

Research prototype

Anvil Sim

A simulation should leave a trail you can inspect.

A Mac workbench for early design screening, bringing geometry, assumptions, simulation results, and review material into one project.

The work

I founded Anvil to connect mechanical engineering with native software. The work spans Swift and Metal interfaces, Rust simulation workflows, and traceable inputs and results.

A choice that matters

Keep the setup, results, and limitations visible together. AI can assist preparation and review; an engineer still makes the decision.

Current scopeSome paths have numerical checks within a defined scope; others remain replay or exploratory workflows. Physical validation and engineering certification are not established.

  • Swift
  • Metal
  • Rust
  • Simulation workflows

02 / Perception × control × hardware

Past engineering project

Autonomous Vehicle

From a camera frame to a moving wheel.

Connecting visual perception, embedded computing, lane following, and steering on a small autonomous vehicle.

The work

My work included lane following on a Jetson Nano and an object-detection pipeline with ROS2, YOLOv5, and OpenCV, connecting vision to real hardware.

A choice that matters

Bring perception and control onto the same vehicle, where the camera, computation, and actuators must work together.

Current scopeThis was a small experimental vehicle, not a demonstration of public-road autonomy.

  • ROS2
  • Jetson Nano
  • YOLOv5
  • OpenCV

03 / Life records × product design

Development prototype

Life Garden

Let the days worth remembering grow into a garden.

An iPhone product experiment connecting a journal, habits, calendar, and focus sessions to a garden that grows over time.

The work

I’m exploring a different interface for keeping a life record: one that lets you revisit a particular day and see what accumulates over time.

A choice that matters

Use the timeline for detail and the garden for accumulation, making a record you want to return to.

Current scopeStill a development prototype. The preview uses fictional records and does not represent a public release.

  • iPhone product design
  • Journaling
  • Interaction design

05 / Global trade × how things are made

iOS internal testing

Pulse

What is the material world behind a trade map?

Follow trade relationships into minerals, materials, processes, and equipment to understand how things reach us.

The work

I’m bringing public data and manufacturing knowledge into a native iOS experience, connecting maps, specific goods, and production processes.

A choice that matters

Keep each observation’s source and date visible, and let a relationship on the map lead into materials and processes.

Current scopeCoverage remains partial. Trade relationships are not physical shipping routes and do not imply live data.

  • SwiftUI
  • Data visualization
  • Public-data provenance
  • Manufacturing systems
