A Star Within the Sleeve

袖中星

A traveler guards a small light through the snow. Beyond the mountains, countless homes hold lights of their own.

ORIGINAL POEM

A Star Within the Sleeve

Jian Qin · Contemporary

万壑沉沉夜未央，

行人袖里护微芒。

星列长空如刻尺，

手中罗盘辨八荒。

风雪弥天犹可度，

人持寸火越寒冈。

千峰万户灯如豆，

忽见人间万点光。

English reading translation

An AI-assisted reading translation for this site. The Chinese text is the original.

Ten thousand valleys lie in darkness; night is not yet over.

A traveler shelters a faint light within his sleeve.

Stars line the vast sky like markings on a ruler;

a compass in his hand finds bearings in the boundless world.

Though wind and snow fill the heavens, the way can still be crossed.

With a tiny flame, he crosses the frozen ridges.

Across a thousand peaks, the lamps of countless homes are small as beans—

and suddenly he sees a world of lights.

READING THE POEM

The world in the words

An original poem and oil painting by Jian Qin. A traveler begins by protecting one small light, finds direction through stars and a compass, and crosses the frozen mountains. The journey moves from solitude toward the discovery of countless illuminated homes.

FROM POEM TO IMAGE

A visual interpretation

The light may be knowledge, conviction, dignity, love, or a freedom someone refuses to surrender. The traveler does not arrive as a savior bringing light to everyone else. He discovers that others already have their own. Independence and human connection can belong in the same landscape.
