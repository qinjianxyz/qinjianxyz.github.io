Privacy policy

Life Garden: Grow Together · Privacy policy for version 1.0.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Your journal, on your device

Memories, photos, private reflection drafts, habits, focus sessions and garden rewards are stored on your device. Life Garden does not operate a journal server or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device backups depend on your iPhone settings. Exported files are copies you control. Deleting the app can remove local records; export anything you want to keep first.

Sharing is your choice

When an iCloud shared space is available and connected, only records you explicitly mark as shared are eligible to sync through Apple iCloud. Invited participants may receive and edit those shared records. Personal journal entries are not included.

Local-only builds do not enable iCloud sharing. In builds with Shared space configured, sharing is optional and uses Apple CloudKit. The app stores your iCloud record identifier and shared-space identifiers locally to check which account owns a connection. Shared records can include their title, notes, dates, tags, selected photo, duration, amount, currency, location text, activity quantities and units (such as water volume or running distance), display name, interaction identifiers, replies and experiment actions when you choose to share them. These are user-entered records; this public build does not import HealthKit data or collect GPS routes. Private reflection drafts, garden rewards and Screen Time selections are not uploaded through this shared journal. Disconnecting keeps downloaded copies on your device and does not revoke the invitation. Use Manage partner invitation to stop access. Copies already received or exported cannot be recalled.

在已配置共享空间的版本中，只有你明确设为共享的记录会通过 Apple iCloud 同步给受邀伴侣。私密反思草稿不会上传。断开连接会保留已下载的副本，并不会撤销邀请；请在「管理伴侣邀请」中停止访问。对方已收到或导出的副本无法收回。

Reflections and shared experiments

Reflections start private. Sharing a saved reflection requires your choice. Experiment invitations, acceptances, pauses and other shared responses are records sent through the same optional shared space. Shared recaps are calculated on your device from records available there; selecting a private recap does not send those records to your partner. The app does not generate a relationship score or send journal content to an AI service.

喝水量、跑步距离、自定义数字与单位、回复和实验互动，仅在相应记录被明确共享时同步。反思默认私密；私密回顾不会自动发给伴侣。本公开版本不读取 HealthKit 或 GPS 轨迹，也不会把日记发送给 AI 服务。

Garden widget

The widget reads only your garden level, sunshine, owned decorations, appearance preference and display status from a local shared container. It does not receive journal titles, photos, money entries or location.

Permissions and purchases

Photos are selected through Apple's picker. This version does not request GPS, contacts, microphone or notification access. Focus timing uses an on-device clock; no whole-phone usage history is collected.

Apple processes App Store purchases. Life Garden checks verified subscription status and does not receive your payment card details. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple account.

Your control

You can edit or delete individual records and export your saved records from You → Export all memories & photos. System backups and any copies you shared may be retained separately by their holders.
