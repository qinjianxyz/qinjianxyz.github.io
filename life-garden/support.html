Support

Support for Life Garden: Grow Together.

Keep your memories safe

Export your journal from You → Export all memories & photos before deleting the app. Exported files preserve your records; automatic import into another installation is not currently offered. Device backups follow your iPhone settings.

Shared memories

In builds with Shared space configured, create a space and invite one partner using Apple’s invitation screen. Each person chooses which memories to mark as shared. Tap Sync shared memories to send and receive changes; synchronization is manual. Personal entries and reflection drafts stay private. If your iCloud account changes, disconnect the old space before connecting another account. Disconnecting retains downloaded copies and does not revoke the invitation; use Manage partner invitation to stop access.

One-week experiments and your story

In Together, invite your partner to try something for one week. Each person accepts independently; an invitation alone is not agreement. Either person can pause or leave. Reflections start private. Continuing or adapting creates a new invitation, with fresh acceptance. The shared story uses completed shared moments, and opens the original records; it does not score your relationship.

Guided private reflections

Closing the guided reflection editor keeps that draft on this device. Other quick-entry editors, including experiment reflections, do not automatically save unfinished text; save before closing. Save privately adds a personal memory to your timeline. If saving fails, keep the editor open and retry. Drafts are not included in journal exports; save a reflection before exporting it. Newer database formats may require a newer app version; avoid downgrading the app.

共享需要双方使用各自的 iCloud 账号并接受邀请，然后手动同步。反思草稿仅保存在本机；私密保存后才会成为可导出的个人记录。保存失败时，请保留编辑页面并重试。

Focus and garden growth

Only active, unpaused time counts. Leaving the app or locking the screen pauses this mode. Return and tap Resume. This build does not block other apps.

Your Home Screen garden

Long-press the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget → Life Garden, then choose Small, Medium or Large. The system controls refresh timing; open Life Garden to publish your latest garden.

Purchases

Use Restore purchases in membership details. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Available offers and prices come from the App Store.

Before contacting support

Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that failed. Do not send passwords, bank details or private journal exports.

English and Mandarin

Choose English or 简体中文 in You. 在「我的」页面选择简体中文。删除应用前，请从「我的」导出需要保留的记录。专注时离开应用或锁屏会暂停成长，回来后点击继续。
